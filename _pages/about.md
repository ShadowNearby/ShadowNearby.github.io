I am a master's student in the [Institute of Parallel and Distributed Systems (IPADS)](https://ipads.se.sjtu.edu.cn/) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Mingkai Dong. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2025.

My research interests span **operating system kernels**, **CXL-based shared memory**, **distributed shared memory systems**, and **mobile AI agent systems**. I am interested in building practical systems that combine principled systems design with emerging hardware and foundation-model capabilities.

## Research interests

- Operating systems and microkernels
- CXL shared memory and distributed shared memory
- Mobile AI agents and task automation

## Education

- **M.Eng. in Computer Science**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2025–present
- **B.Eng. in Computer Science**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2021–2025
