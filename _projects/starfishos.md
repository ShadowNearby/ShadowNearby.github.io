StarfishOS revisits the single-system-image abstraction for CXL-connected machines. Its state-partitioned microkernel architecture is designed to combine system-wide resource management with clear ownership and scalable coordination across nodes.

The work will appear at the **ACM Symposium on Operating Systems Principles (SOSP 2026)**.
